Catalog Janitor

Catalog Janitor checks every product in your Shopify store and shows you what's wrong: duplicate products, copies left over from the Duplicate button, the same SKU on two items, products with no image, empty SEO titles and descriptions, sale badges on items that aren't on sale, and tags spelled three different ways.

Then it fixes the ones that can be fixed safely, in bulk, after you've seen exactly what will change. Every fix is saved with the old values so you can put them back with one click.

What it checks

DuplicatesSame title, copies with -1 or -copy handles, shared SKUs and barcodes
PricingVariants priced at 0, compare-at prices that aren't above the price
ImagesProducts with no image, images with no alt text
SEOMissing, too long or repeated SEO titles and descriptions
ContentEmpty or very thin descriptions
Tags"Sale", "sale" and "SALE " on different products
DataMissing SKU, vendor or product type, drafts that have stock

Safe by design

Nothing changes until you press Apply. Duplicates are archived, not deleted, so they leave your storefront but stay in your admin. The History page keeps every batch of changes with the old values and an Undo button.
