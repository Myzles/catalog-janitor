Privacy policy

Last updated 17 November 2026. Catalog Janitor is run by Swag Studios (contact swagstudios.helpdesk@outlook.com).

What we store

  • Your store's myshopify.com address, your plan, and how many fixes you made this month.
  • The change history: for each fix, the product and media IDs, product titles, and the old and new value of the field that changed (status, SEO title or description, alt text, tags or compare-at price). This is what makes Undo possible.
  • Feedback you send from inside the app.

What we do not store

The scan runs in your browser. Your product catalog is exported by Shopify and checked on your device; we do not keep a copy. We never access customer or order data and the app does not request those permissions. We do not sell or share data or use it for advertising or model training.

Who handles data

  • Cloudflare, which hosts the app and its database (United States).
  • Shopify, where your store data lives and which handles billing.

How long we keep it

Change history is kept while the app is installed. When you uninstall, we delete everything about your store right away, and again when Shopify sends its shop data deletion request.

Security

All requests use HTTPS and are checked against Shopify's signed session tokens. We do not store Shopify access tokens.

Your rights

Email us for a copy of your data or to delete it.
