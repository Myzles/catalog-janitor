Common questions

Does it delete products? No. Duplicate copies are set to Archived. You can delete them yourself in Shopify later, or restore them from History.

How long does a scan take? Shopify exports the catalog for us. Small stores take a few seconds; 50,000 products take a couple of minutes.

Which product is kept in a duplicate set? The active one first, then the one with more images, then the longer description, then the oldest.

Can I undo a fix? Yes. Open History and press Undo on the batch. Undo doesn't count against the free plan.

Why isn't there a fix button for missing images or descriptions? Those need your own photos and words, so the app lists them and links each product.

Billing. Pro is billed through your Shopify invoice. Cancel by switching to Free in the app's plan page or by uninstalling.
